# What does the 31.6% result actually show?

**For:** business analysts, data scientists, strategy consultants  
**Timebox:** 30 minutes  
**Tools:** Spreadsheet/Python/SQL and AI agents are allowed. Do not contact people for this exercise.

## Task

Assess whether the evidence supports the impact note’s claim that the pilot reduced DSM penalty by 31.6%. State the strongest conclusion you can defend and what you cannot infer.

## Inputs in scope

- `data/reports/AI_Pilot_Impact_Note.md`
- `data/operations/dispatch_blocks.csv`
- `data/process/DSM_Commercial_Extract.pdf`

## Submit

A one-page judgment with an evidence table (claim | source | confidence), rejected hypotheses with reasons, and one recommended next measurement.

Every submission must include an **evidence table** with `claim | source | confidence`, plus the **hypotheses you rejected and why**. If the brief is underdetermined, reframing it, identifying what is missing, and acting safely under uncertainty counts as success. Do not assume every oddity is meaningful.
