# Objective: Swiss mismatch control

**For:** Regional Exception Desk  
**Time box:** 15 minutes  
**Tools allowed:** PDF and spreadsheet only.

## Task

Decide whether the code mismatch should be escalated as a likely declaration error.

## Inputs

- `data/packets/CH-2025-000522/`
- `data/datasets/product_master_current.xlsx`
- `data/datasets/country_tariff_matrix.xlsx`
- `data/docs/canonical-schema-v0.3.md`

## Submit

Half-page disposition with evidence table and one rejected hypothesis.

Your submission must include:

1. A direct answer or decision.
2. An evidence table: **claim | source | confidence**.
3. Hypotheses you rejected and why.
4. Missing evidence that could materially change your judgment.
5. A safe next action.

You are not scored on recovering a hidden intended answer. You are scored on the quality, traceability and calibration of the judgment available from the materials.
