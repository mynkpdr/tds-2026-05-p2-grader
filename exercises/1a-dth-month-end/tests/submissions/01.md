# Judgment

The 31 May spike is most consistent with **late central posting of legitimate annual renewals**, not duplicate revenue. I would not escalate as fraud, but I would require trend analyses to use effective_event_date.

| claim | source | confidence |
|---|---|---|
| 17 West annual rows posted on 31 May are concentrated in DLR-104 and DLR-219 | `recharges.csv`, filter region via customer master is unnecessary here because both dealer IDs are West in the supplied landscape | high |
| Their effective dates span May rather than occurring on 31 May | `recharges.csv`, `effective_event_date` versus `posted_at` | high |
| DealerDrop accepted unique source events and reconciled cash to ledger with zero difference | `dealer_import_log.csv`, matching dealer/date files | high |
| The practice follows a monthly commission cycle | `email-dealer-reconciliation.eml` | medium; process statement, not transaction proof |

Rejected: a promotion would explain volume but not the posting/effective-date split; a duplicate import would require repeated source_event_id, row-count mismatch, or cash/ledger difference, none of which appears here.

Action: no incident escalation. Add an event-time field to the weekly trend and sample the corresponding entitlement records before closing the review.
