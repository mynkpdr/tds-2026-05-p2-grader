# Month-end mystery

**Your role:** Retention analytics manager  
**Timebox:** 10 minutes  
**Tools:** Spreadsheet, SQL, or an AI coding agent; no interviews in the first 10 minutes.  
**Submit:** A one-page smell test with evidence table, rejected hypotheses, and a go/no-go recommendation for escalation.

## Objective

A dashboard reviewer flags an unusual cluster of West-region annual renewals on 31 May. Decide whether the pattern indicates duplicate revenue, manipulated retention reporting, a data-pipeline defect, or no material issue based on the supplied evidence.

## Available material

- `data/datasets/recharges.csv`
- `data/datasets/dealer_import_log.csv`
- `data/communications/email-dealer-reconciliation.eml`

## Required in your submission

- A direct judgment or recommendation.
- An evidence table: `claim | source | confidence`.
- Hypotheses you rejected and why.
- What remains unknown and what evidence would change your decision.
- A safe next action under the current uncertainty.

You are not graded on finding a hidden answer. You are graded on the quality, traceability, calibration, and usefulness of your judgment from the evidence available.
