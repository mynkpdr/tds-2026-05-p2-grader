# Alternative judgment

The pilot may have improved self-service **and** weakened measurement. The email reports fewer transfers, and many balance-check or pack-information sessions resolve without cases. At the same time, error/abandonment before case_create rose and service-event exposure did not improve. The available evidence cannot allocate the net effect.

I would retain the pilot while randomizing a small callback sample across resolved, abandoned, and error sessions. National expansion should depend on verified resolution and repeat-contact rates, not CareDesk volume.
