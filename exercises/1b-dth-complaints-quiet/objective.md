# Complaints went quiet

**Your role:** Forward-deployed analyst embedded with customer care  
**Timebox:** 30 minutes  
**Tools:** Any local analysis tool or AI agent. You may choose one person from data/people to question; the instructor role-plays them.  
**Submit:** A concise diagnostic note naming the person you would question and the exact five questions, plus evidence and rejected hypotheses.

## Objective

South-region complaint cases fell after the NovaIVR pilot. Assess whether this is evidence that self-service improved customer experience and whether the pilot should expand nationally.

## Available material

- `data/datasets/tickets.csv`
- `data/datasets/ivr_interactions.jsonl`
- `data/datasets/service_events.csv`
- `data/communications/email-ivr-pilot.eml`
- `data/people/farah-iqbal.md`
- `data/people/dev-khanna.md`

## Required in your submission

- A direct judgment or recommendation.
- An evidence table: `claim | source | confidence`.
- Hypotheses you rejected and why.
- What remains unknown and what evidence would change your decision.
- A safe next action under the current uncertainty.

You are not graded on finding a hidden answer. You are graded on the quality, traceability, calibration, and usefulness of your judgment from the evidence available.
