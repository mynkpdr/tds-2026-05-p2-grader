# Ten-minute smell test: inverter event export

**For:** all analyst profiles  
**Timebox:** 10 minutes  
**Tools:** Any local inspection tool or AI agent is allowed. No other files are in scope.

## Task

Does anything here smell wrong enough to escalate? You are allowed to conclude “no.” Identify at most two things worth checking and explain why you would or would not spend more time.

## Inputs in scope

- `data/operations/inverter_events.csv`

## Submit

Five bullets maximum, including evidence and confidence. Also list one hypothesis you reject.

Every submission must include an **evidence table** with `claim | source | confidence`, plus the **hypotheses you rejected and why**. If the brief is underdetermined, reframing it, identifying what is missing, and acting safely under uncertainty counts as success. Do not assume every oddity is meaningful.
