# Objective: Is the Irish preference claim supported?

**For:** Ireland Trade Specialist  
**Time box:** 45 minutes  
**Tools allowed:** PDF, CSV and optional AI assistant.

## Task

Assess whether the preferential-origin claim is supported by the available evidence and what action is safe now.

## Inputs

- `data/packets/IE-2025-000411/`
- `data/datasets/supplier_origin_register.csv`
- `data/support/meeting-transcripts/origin-workshop.md`

## Submit

Decision note with evidence table, confidence, rejected hypotheses and missing evidence request.

Your submission must include:

1. A direct answer or decision.
2. An evidence table: **claim | source | confidence**.
3. Hypotheses you rejected and why.
4. Missing evidence that could materially change your judgment.
5. A safe next action.

You are not scored on recovering a hidden intended answer. You are scored on the quality, traceability and calibration of the judgment available from the materials.
