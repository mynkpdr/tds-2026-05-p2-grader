# Blind evaluator prompt: Is the Irish preference claim supported?

You receive this objective, the analyst-visible inputs, the instructor-supplied dynamics and one submission. You do **not** receive the test expectations or other submissions.

Evaluate the submission independently.

## Mandatory order

1. Extract every material claim and cited source.
2. Verify whether each cited source supports the claim and record limitations.
3. Identify hypotheses tested, rejected or ignored.
4. Score the **evidence and reasoning first, then the conclusion**.
5. A well-supported alternative conclusion can score as high as the designed conclusion.
6. The intended conclusion asserted without evidence must not score high.
7. Tone, omission, timing and silence may suggest hypotheses, but treat them as evidence only when corroborated.
8. Do not reward suspicion for its own sake or penalize a justified 'insufficient evidence' conclusion.
9. Cite exact text from the submission when explaining every score.

## Exercise-specific success condition

Distinguish manufacturing country from preferential origin and avoid declaring fraud or validity without the supplier evidence.

Relevant dynamics: D4.

## Dimensions: score each 0-4

- **Evidence traceability (evidence)**: Claims are tied to specific files, rows, events or passages; source limitations are stated.
- **Reasoning and hypothesis testing (reasoning)**: Competing explanations are tested and rejected only when evidence warrants.
- **Calibration (calibration)**: Confidence and uncertainty match the strength of evidence; missing data is identified precisely.
- **Process and system understanding (systems)**: The response explains how process, incentives, data lineage and system behavior produce the observed evidence.
- **Decision safety (action)**: Recommended actions are proportionate, reversible where needed and avoid unsupported operational or compliance claims.

## Behavioural anchors

- **4**: Specific, traceable evidence; tests alternatives; calibrated; explains mechanism; safe and actionable.
- **3**: Mostly evidence-based with minor gaps; alternatives considered; conclusion and action generally proportionate.
- **2**: Some relevant evidence but incomplete tracing or mechanism; confidence too high/low; action needs safeguards.
- **1**: Mostly restates the brief, relies on one clue or tone, weak sourcing, unsupported certainty or unsafe action.
- **0**: No usable analysis, fabricated evidence, or conclusion contradicts supplied materials without explanation.

## Output format

- Overall judgment band: Exceptional / Strong / Mixed / Weak / Unusable
- Dimension scores with exact quoted evidence from the submission
- Best-supported conclusion
- Material unsupported claims
- Missing or mishandled alternative explanations
- Whether the proposed action is safe
- One sentence describing what would most improve the submission
