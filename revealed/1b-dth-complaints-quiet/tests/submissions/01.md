# Judgment

The decline in South CareDesk cases is **not sufficient evidence of improved experience**. It is consistent with a case-capture break before authentication, but IVR abandonment alone is not a complaint.

| claim | source | confidence |
|---|---|---|
| South case creation falls abruptly after 10 May; other regions do not show the same break | `tickets.csv` | high |
| South service events do not decline in parallel | `service_events.csv` | medium; exposure is estimated |
| NovaIVR shows more sessions ending at authenticate/issue_menu with ERROR or ABANDONED and no case_id | `ivr_interactions.jsonl` | high for telemetry, medium for customer intent |
| The pilot dashboard excludes pre-authentication sessions | `email-ivr-pilot.eml` | high |

I would question Farah Iqbal: At which stage is a case created? What authentication defects remained at launch? How many failed sessions repeat within 24 hours? Are repeat attempts linked across ANI/token changes? What do sampled recordings or callbacks say about unresolved intent?

Recommendation: keep the bounded pilot, do not expand from case counts, and create an end-to-end journey outcome linking IVR sessions, repeat contacts, cases, and sampled resolution.
